# Northwind Trading Analysis  
## Problem Identification: Qualitative and Quantitative Analysis  

This section presents the qualitative and quantitative analysis conducted to understand the decline in Northwind Trading’s online sales performance. The analysis compares February and March metrics, reviews customer complaints, and evaluates the store manager’s report to identify patterns and key issues affecting sales.

### Quantitative Analysis Summary
A comparison of February and March sales performance shows a significant decline in revenue. Total sales value dropped, and the number of transactions reduced noticeably. Average daily sales also decreased, indicating that fewer purchases were made each day. Although the average transaction value declined slightly, the major driver of the revenue drop was the reduction in completed transactions.

### Customer Complaints Summary
Customer feedback following the website redesign revealed that most complaints were related to the checkout process and mandatory registration requirement. Users reported that the process was complex and time-consuming. Additional concerns included website navigation issues, payment options, and website speed. These challenges likely contributed to fewer completed purchases.

### Store Manager Report Evaluation
The store manager indicated that daily sales fluctuations were normal. However, further analysis showed a clear decline in overall performance compared to February. While daily variations existed, the average number of transactions and total sales were significantly lower. The findings suggest that the decline is linked to the new checkout process rather than normal fluctuations.

### Key Insight
The decline in revenue is primarily driven by fewer completed transactions. Customer complaints and performance metrics both indicate that the new checkout process and mandatory registration created friction in the user journey.

### Conclusion
The website redesign and mandatory registration requirement negatively impacted customer experience. This led to a reduction in daily transactions and ultimately lower revenue. The main issue is the drop in transaction volume rather than a significant change in customer spending behavior.
