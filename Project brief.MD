# Northwind Trading eCommerce Experience Analysis
### Business Analysis Case Study  

## Project Description

This project is a Business Analysis case study focused on investigating a sudden decline in online sales and rising customer dissatisfaction at **Northwind Trading**, an eCommerce company selling office supplies.  

The case study was completed in **partial fulfillment of the Microsoft Certified Business Analyst course** and was approached as a real-world BA engagement. The goal is to identify what went wrong after a recent website redesign and provide actionable recommendations to ensure stable revenue and improve customer experience.  

---

## Background

Northwind Trading has operated successfully for four years, with stable sales and expected seasonal fluctuations. The company’s website serves as its primary revenue channel.  

Recently, the company recorded a **decline in monthly sales for the first time** and received negative feedback from customers on social media about the online store.  

One month prior, Northwind Trading redesigned its website, introducing a **mandatory customer registration** step before checkout. This change was implemented to collect customer data for marketing and track purchase history.  

While the website remained functional, users experienced slower page loads and a more complex checkout journey. Management became concerned that these changes may have impacted sales and customer satisfaction.  

---

## Full Problem Statement

Northwind Trading has recorded a **decline in online sales** and an **increase in negative customer feedback** in the past month, marking the first downward trend in four years of operation. This change **coincides with a recent website redesign** that introduced mandatory customer registration at checkout.  

The company is concerned that these changes may negatively affect **customer experience and revenue**. An investigation is required to determine the **root causes** of the decline and the **extent of the impact**.  

---

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to **analyze Northwind Trading’s online sales performance and customer experience following the website redesign, identify the root causes of the decline in sales and negative customer feedback, and develop actionable recommendations to enhance customer satisfaction and business performance.**

---

## Scope of Analysis

## Scope of Analysis

This project involves a **comprehensive Business Analysis** of Northwind Trading following a website redesign. The analysis includes both quantitative and qualitative investigation, as well as stakeholder and user role assessment. Key focus areas include:

### Quantitative and Qualitative Analysis
- Scrutinize March sales data to identify trends: growing, declining, or stable sales, and website visits.  
- Calculate March sales totals, average daily sales, number of transactions per day, and average transaction value per period.  
- Compare February and March sales totals and calculate the difference in value and percentage.  
- Analyze customer complaints, categorize recurring themes, and count complaints per category.  
- Review the Sales Manager’s report and evaluate agreement with the analysis.  

### Root Cause Analysis
- Identify questions to determine the root cause of declining sales and negative customer feedback.  
- Apply the **5 Whys method** with fictional stakeholder responses to demonstrate understanding.  
- Provide recommendations to management backed by analysis results.  

### Users and Stakeholders
- Conduct **User Role Analysis** to understand different types of website users.  
- Perform **Stakeholder Analysis and Mapping** on a Power–Interest Grid to prioritize engagement.  
- Develop a **RACI Matrix** to allocate tasks and responsibilities for the website redesign project.  

This analysis ensures a **holistic understanding** of the business problem, its impact on users and stakeholders, and actionable recommendations to improve customer experience and sales performance.
